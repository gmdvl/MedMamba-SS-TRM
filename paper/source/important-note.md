* Don't invent experimental results.
* Don't invent datasets.
* Don't invent hyperparameters.
* Don't invent architectural components.
* Don't claim an experiment was performed if it isn't supported by the files.
* Don't fabricate citations.
* Don't turn assumptions into facts.
* If information is missing, explicitly mark it as missing.

Write as a human researcher, not as an AI assistant.

The writing should be academically rigorous but natural. Avoid repetitive sentence structures, excessive section summaries, generic introductions, unnecessary adjectives, and artificial phrases such as "It is important to note that", "Furthermore", "In conclusion", and similar filler.

Vary sentence length naturally. Use precise technical terminology where appropriate, but do not unnecessarily complicate sentences.

Explain why methodological decisions were made rather than merely listing them.

When discussing experimental results, interpret the results rather than simply repeating numerical values.

Distinguish clearly between observed results, interpretations, hypotheses, and conclusions.

Do not exaggerate the significance of the results.

If the results are weak, unstable, contradictory, or inconclusive, report that honestly and explain possible reasons.

Maintain terminology consistently throughout the paper.
